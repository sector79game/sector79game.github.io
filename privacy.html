Sector 79

Privacy Policy · Effective 1 November 2026

This policy covers the Sector 79 app for iPhone and iPad.

What the app collects

Nothing. The app does not collect, store on a server, sell, or share any personal information. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind.

What stays on your device

Your settings, scores, streak, service record and saved runs are kept only on your own device, inside the app. They are never sent to us. Deleting the app deletes them.

Purchases

The optional league unlock is sold through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment; we never see your name, payment details or Apple Account. The app only asks your device's App Store whether the unlock has been bought, so it can open the leagues.

Children

The app collects no information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If the app ever starts collecting any data (for example, a worldwide leaderboard), this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the change will be listed here with its date.

I bought the league unlock. How do I get it on another iPhone or iPad?

Sign in with the same Apple Account and open the app; it restores itself. If it doesn't, tap ABOUT on the title screen, then RESTORE PURCHASES.

The screen is sideways

The game plays in landscape only. Turn your device sideways.

No sound

Check the ringer switch or Action button and the volume, and that SOUND is ON in the pause menu.

Turn vibration off

Pause during a flight, or open ABOUT on the title screen, and tap VIBRATION.

Privacy

The app collects no data. Read the privacy policy.
